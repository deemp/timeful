Root cause of the collapsed-hours grey bullet:

- `markCollapsedRowInactive` (`useTimedGridInteractions.ts:230-238`) calls `opts.markCurTimeslotInactive?.()` (`useAvailabilityData.ts:703-714`, sets `curTimeslotCellState.value = null`) AND then `opts.clearSelectedSlot?.()` (nulls it again). Final state null → grey.
