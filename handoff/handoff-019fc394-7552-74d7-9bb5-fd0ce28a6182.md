## Important Decisions

- FR-026 is authoritative for timed-grid column ownership and axis behavior. Columns represent date-picker picked dates in the event timezone; changing `Shown in` only changes displayed clock time and tooltip date/time.
- Specific-times editing requires all increments from `00:00` inclusive to the following `00:00` exclusive to be enabled for every picked date. Active slots are the selected subset.
- Saved specific-times grids show their complete enabled domain, with inactive spans eligible for collapse. Enabled inactive cells are not padding and are not respondent-selectable.
- Range events retain only selected-range slots as enabled and active. Their axis is hour-aligned; any rendered non-enabled boundary cell is outside the selected range, not padding.
- A DST fall-back repeated local-clock time must render as two distinct, chronologically ordered slots with offset or timezone-abbreviation labels. A spring-forward nonexistent local-clock time must not render.
- Wrapped ranges remain continuous without a structural split gap.
